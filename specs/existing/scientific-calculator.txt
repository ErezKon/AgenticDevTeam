for the existing SimpleCalculator program, 
I want to add scientific calculator functionality.
The calculator should support the following operations:
- Square root (√)
- Power (x^y)
- Logarithm (log)
- Natural logarithm (ln)
- Sine (sin)
- Cosine (cos)
- Tangent (tan)
- Factorial (n!)
- Pi (π)
- E (e)
- Absolute value (|x|)
- Radians to degrees (rad→deg)
- Degrees to radians (deg→rad)
- Percentage (%)
- Parentheses ()
- Decimal numbers (.)
- Negative numbers (-)

I want the operations to be presented on the screen in a scientific calculator format.
I want the operations to be grouped in a logical way, such as:
- Basic operations (addition, subtraction, multiplication, division)
- Scientific operations (square root, power, logarithm, natural logarithm, sine, cosine, tangent, factorial, pi, e, absolute value, radians to degrees, degrees to radians, percentage)
- Additional operations (parentheses, decimal numbers, negative numbers)

Also I want them to be represented with their symbol, not their name, and have a tooltip that shows the name when hovered over.
